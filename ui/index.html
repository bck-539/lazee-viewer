Faces

Faces are found and compared on this PC only; nothing is sent anywhere. The scan runs in the background after new photos are read, and can be paused like any scan. Turning this off deletes all face data.

Models are plug-ins: put a model folder with a model.toml in and it shows up here. Switching models looks at every photo again.
